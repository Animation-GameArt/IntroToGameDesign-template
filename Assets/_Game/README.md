# _Game — your files

This folder is yours. Put everything you make for your own game here: scenes, art, audio, prefabs and scripts.

Course updates never change this folder.

## Credits

`Credits` is the text shown on your game's Credits screen. Put your name and your game's title in it, and add an entry every time you add an asset you didn't make: its name, who made it, where it came from, and its license.
