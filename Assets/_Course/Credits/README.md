# Credits

`Course Credits` lists everything that comes with the course project: the scripts, fonts and any art or sound packs. It is shown on the game's Credits screen.

Your own credits go in `Assets/_Game/Credits`. Add a line there every time you add an asset you didn't make.

**For the instructor:** when an art or sound pack is added under `Assets/_Course/Art`, keep the pack's own license file in its folder, and add the pack to `Course Credits` with its name, its author and its license.
