# 3D Scripts

Scripts for 3D and 2.5D games. They are used in the 3D course project; this copy is the master copy, kept with the rest of `_Course`.

| Script | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Movement/TankController3D` | A player turned by left and right and moved by forward and back, with jumping. For a camera behind the player |
| `Movement/RailRunner3D` | Keeps the player on a spline rail they run along, step sideways on and jump from |

The tutorials on the course site, **2.5D Tutorial 1 — Third-Person Controller** and **2.5D Tutorial 2 — Rail Runner**, show how to use them.
